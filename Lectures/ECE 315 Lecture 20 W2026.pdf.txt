Some Motivation
· Embedded systems must reliably communicate with sensors, actuators, and peripherals
· Interfaces often fail due to design, electrical, timing, or integration errors
· Early planning + test-friendly design reduces time-to-market and hardware iterations (board spins)
· Hardware iterations are expensive and time-bound
· Software/firmware is easy and quick to change, update, but still costs

Joint Test Action Group (JTAG)

· An industry standard for verifying designs of and testing printed circuit boards after manufacture
· Often built into chips
· Used as the primary means of accessing sub-blocks of integrated circuits, otherwise they are not accessible
· Used to program internal flash, such as found on Zynq and RP2040
· Can verify connectivity of chips, critical for ball-grid array (BGA) devices that can have hundreds of connections
· Best Practice:
· Have JTAG connector for prototypes, keep pads for production just in case

Built-in Self-Test (BIST)
· Typical approaches:
· Memory self-test · Peripheral loopback (internal/external) · Analogue self-test (comparator checks, ADC ramp tests) · Pattern generation for I/O pins
· This is where the test point pads and use of pogo-pins or flying probes comes in
· States and results are logged to FLASH or output via UART, or both
· Best Practices:
· Like JTAG, have UART header that is optional to populate · Use LEDs to signal status (e.g., use combination of state and patterns)

Certifications
· We see everyday marks on products like UL, CE, FCC · Various organizations exist to regulate products and
systems for safety and commercial reasons · Responsibility of the designer and engineers to
ensure that products and systems are compliant · This always costs in time and money; wise to think of from the start · Plan for compliance pre- and final-testing, again, time and cost
· Pre-testing saves money and time, de-risks designs

· Plan interface requirements early · Integrate DFT features at PCB and firmware levels · Always include test points, headers, and ground access · Add self-test routines · Validate interfaces using both hardware and software tools · Maintain documentation and schematics updated for test teams
