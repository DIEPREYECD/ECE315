Explain why excess computational capacity is desirable in real-time system design. This refers to more than idle time.

Your Answer:

Excess computational capacity is desired in real-time systems because real-time systems must be able to meet deadlines under the worst-case scenarios and unexpected event loads. This helps it cancel out time variability, prevent missing deadlines, and allows the real-time system to function properly.
